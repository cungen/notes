不顺心的时候 你可以像疯狗一样发狂

You can be as mad as

a mad dog at the way things went.


可以破口大骂诅咒命运

You could swear, curse the Fates.


可到头来

But when it comes to the end


还是得任它而去

you have to let go.


-- 返老还童
